Video Tag

The HTML <video> tag is used to natively embed video media player content directly into a webpage without requiring third-party plugins.

<video width="300" controls autoplay loop>
<source src="./video/video1.mp4" type="video/mp4">
Your browser does not support the HTML video tag.
</video>

Iframe Tag

An <iframe> (Inline Frame) is an HTML element used to embed another document or webpage within your current webpage. It functions as a window that displays external content (like a YouTube video, a Google Map, or another HTML file) without requiring the user to leave your site

Audio Tag
